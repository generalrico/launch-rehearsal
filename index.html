How the Telegram review works
On the review screen, copy the setup and paste it in your private Telegram group. Tag your Switch agent, then have a teammate approve or request changes in the same chat. Return here to simulate the approved setup.
Give your Switch agent these instructions:
When tagged with a Launch Rehearsal setup, check that supply and allocation are positive whole numbers, allocation does not exceed supply, price is positive, and demand is a nonnegative whole number. Calculate expected sales and mock proceeds. Flag missing or invalid values and ask for corrections. If the setup passes, ask a human reviewer to approve or request changes.
